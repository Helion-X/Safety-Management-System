Safety Report — Security Report
Reporting is the foundation of aviation safety. Every event you report — even one that seems minor or that ended without consequence — lets us see a risk before it turns into an accident. What you write here is treated in confidence and used to improve our operation, never to assign blame. The few minutes you spend on this report may one day save the life of a colleague.
